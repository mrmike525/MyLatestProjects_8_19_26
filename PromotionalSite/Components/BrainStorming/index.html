I am an aspiring Front-End Software Developer, lifelong technology enthusiast, and Minnesota Army National Guard combat veteran. I proudly served in Operation Iraqi Freedom from January 2006 through September 2007.

My professional and personal experience with technology spans more than 30 years, beginning with building desktop computers in 1995. From 1998 to 2002, I provided computer repair and technical support for my high school, developing a strong foundation in hardware troubleshooting and problem-solving.

I continued building my technical expertise through positions with Apple Technical Support, serving in both Tier 1 and Tier 2 roles from 2008 to 2010. From 2017 to 2019, I worked as a Senior Technician specializing in diagnosing and repairing iPhones, iPads, Macs, and Android smartphones, including Google Pixel devices.

More recently, I shifted my focus toward software development. In 2021, I completed a Software QA Engineering program through Careerist, gaining foundational knowledge of software testing and quality assurance.

I subsequently earned a certificate in Enterprise Software Development through Springboard, completing an intensive, front-end-focused program covering HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Git, and GitHub.

Today, I continue expanding my software development skills through independent study and hands-on projects, applying my extensive technical background to building functional, responsive, and user-friendly web applications.
